# Fonts

- Caveat variable: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat — downloaded from the official raw repository; license Caveat-OFL.txt. Used for handwritten artist cards and the underground note.
- Manrope variable: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope — downloaded from the official raw repository; license Manrope-OFL.txt. Used for body text and music controls.
- Existing Cormorant Garamond: OFL.txt. Used for small display headings.

All fonts are local. The three large artist wordmarks are original drawn paths in src/lettering.js.
